Mentor signature for the endorsed architecture (Elevem-se = method; Maycko & Marcela = the mentors who sign it). Serif uppercase form, brand colours — Marcela dourado, Maycko vinho.

```jsx
<Signature mentor="couple" tone="wine" />
<Signature mentor="marcela" role />
<Signature mentor="maycko" tone="light" size="lg" />
```

- `couple` is the joint "Maycko & Marcela" lockup (gold italic ampersand) — use it as the endorsement under the Elevem-se logo, in footers and closings.
- Individual mentors carry their own colour; `tone="light"` on wine/dark backgrounds shifts them to gold/ivory.
- Never recolour to anything outside wine/gold — the old navy-blue signature is retired.
